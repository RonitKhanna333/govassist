03 · Next

What is left

Before wider use

  • Independent review of each rule pack. The app shows "not yet reviewed" until then
  • Split the PMFME district-list question into its two routes and add "not sure"
  • A regression test that checks explanations against the answers collected
  • A retention policy for call transcripts

Later

  • Mic input for Punjabi and Tamil
  • Testing with real applicants
  • Load and accessibility measurements
  • ITR and GST, document upload and state schemes
